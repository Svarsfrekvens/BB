# Flora: local duty-domain experiment, 2026-10-21

This branch adds an opt-in **experimental** `EXPLICIT_NO_BREAK_SCENARIO` mode to the Flora method. The existing solver-placed-break mode remains the default and its saved results are untouched. The experiment models active-only candidate duties from 4 through 13 hours, including 6, 7, and 8 hours. A combined work/on-call/work duty is a separate whole duty: its overall span is not cut to the active-only 13-hour limit.

In the new mode, source breaks must be absent, the solver generates no breaks, ordinary targets must be supplied in the same no-break minute accounting, and any new active-only candidate longer than 13 hours is rejected. The independent validator rejects inserted breaks and active-only duties above 13 hours. The run is identified as `joint-shifts-no-break-experiment/v0`; no-break applicability is reported as unknown for contractual/legal review, not silently approved.

Local synthetic tests cover 4, 6, 7, 8, and 13-hour active-only duties without breaks; 3 and 14 hours are rejected. A fixed 24-hour work/on-call/work duty remains intact and without a generated break. This branch also adds an **optional**, exactly timed 24-hour `Ar–Jo–Ar` candidate as one indivisible solver decision. Its active parts count toward customer capacity and ordinary/temporary active minutes; the `Jo` part counts separately toward on-call time and its rolling cap. All three parts share one generated pass identity and are checked together by the independent validator. The candidate requires a sleep-on-call-qualified person and at least five hours of contiguous night on-call. Historical approved pass identities are source evidence only, and sick-time rows never become work or on-call.

The importer accepts such candidates only from an explicit, sourced `LOCAL_MODEL_EXPERIMENT` profile entry. It never generates them automatically from the approved historical source or the browser workspace. To keep aggregate capacity accounting exact, this first experiment rejects possible active-work windows that overlap for the same employee; broader competing candidate domains require a separate encoding. No future Flora duty dates or employee availability have been inferred from the historic roster.

Two local probes used the same saved Flora request and the reviewed historical-pass profile, copied into memory. Both removed the profile's mathematical reference breaks and recomputed ordinary targets from source `Ar` gross minutes; neither changed the installed profile or sent a job to the remote engine. The first kept the existing 55 candidate windows; the second widened 54 windows from a 10.5-hour maximum to 13 hours within their existing clock windows. The one more restricted window was retained.

| Probe | Covered source needs | Ordinary active minutes | Temporary active minutes | Break minutes | Independent checked status |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| No-break; original candidate windows | 1,451 / 1,451 | 30,930 | 8,275 | 0 | PASS |
| No-break; 54 widened windows | 1,451 / 1,451 | 30,930 | 8,450 | 0 | PASS |

Both searches returned `FEASIBLE` within a 12-second budget per objective stage, **not** an optimum for temporary time. The temporary-minute totals must not be interpreted as a proven benefit or loss from widening windows. They are also not directly comparable with the old break-deducting result. Those probes did not include new optional whole-duty candidates; they test the active-only duration and no-break accounting. Whole-duty selection has passed synthetic tests, but a real Flora comparison needs a separate explicit future candidate domain and availability source.

## Historical Flora replay

`motor/experiments/replay_flora_approved_duties.py` performs a local holdout replay using paths to the saved request, reviewed profile and clean historical-example catalog. It does not submit an engine job or modify any input file. The script removes 15 clean P2 duties from the fixed source schedule and offers the **same person and exact source times** back as optional candidates: eleven 24-hour `Ar–Jo–Ar` whole duties and four 11-hour active-only duties. G24 starts in P1 and remains fixed history. The eight sick-affected source groups are not converted to candidates.

The independent result check passed: 13 of 15 held-out duties were selected, all 1,451 source needs were mathematically covered, ordinary active time was 30,930 minutes, temporary active time 6,765 minutes, on-call time 11,070 minutes, and generated break time was zero. G02 and G13 were not selected in this feasible result. Coverage stages were optimal, but the temporary-time stage was only `FEASIBLE` within a 20-second search budget, so the selected set is not an optimality claim. The seven remaining unknown checks include no-break applicability, full daily-rest boundary evidence, and contract accounting. The replay shows the candidate encoding can use real Flora source rows; it does not determine a future roster or prove that omitting breaks is contractually allowed.

Verification: all 511 motor tests passed for the final branch (6 skipped), including focused checks for complete selection, pass identity, break-free accounting, night on-call eligibility, rolling on-call cap, sick-time rejection, and rejection of a tampered partial result. No live profile, saved motor result, business workspace, or published app was modified.
